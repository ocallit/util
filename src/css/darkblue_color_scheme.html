Dark Blue Color Scheme

Enhanced Color Properties:

Surface Color: #ffffff (white) - clean background for cards, modals, and content areas

Text Colors: Dark gray (#212529), medium gray (#6c757d), light gray (#adb5bd)

Border Colors: Light gray (#dee2e6) and dark gray (#adb5bd)

Elevation System:

Level 1: Subtle shadow for slight elevation

Level 2: Medium shadow for cards and panels

Level 3: Strong shadow for modals and overlays

Tinted Elevations: Primary and accent color variations for themed components
